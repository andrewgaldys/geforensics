Services

Clear answers to hard financial questions

Whether you are dividing a marital estate, investigating suspected fraud, or preparing for litigation, we trace the money and organize the evidence so you and your attorney can move forward with confidence.

Divorce Litigation Support

Trace marital and separate funds, identify undisclosed income and assets, and compare lifestyle spending against reported income.

Asset Tracing

Follow funds across bank, brokerage, and business accounts to identify unexplained transfers and where the money ultimately went.

Bank & Brokerage Analysis

Review account activity to reconstruct cash flows, flag irregular transactions, and build clear, documented timelines.

Business Records Review

Analyze business books and records for diverted revenue, personal expenses run through the business, and unreported income.

Fraud & Tax Matters

Examine embezzlement, misappropriation, and tax-related financial issues, drawing on years of federal criminal investigative experience.

Discovery & Deposition Support

Organize financial evidence, interview witnesses, pinpoint gaps in discovery, and help counsel prepare for depositions.

How we work

A disciplined process from first call to final findings

  1. Confidential consultation

    Tell us about the matter, the questions you need answered, and the records available.

  2. Defined scope

    We agree on objectives, sources, and deliverables up front, often working at the direction of your attorney.

  3. Investigation & analysis

    We trace transactions, reconstruct cash flows, and test what the records show against what has been reported.

  4. Clear findings

    You receive organized, well-documented findings, including schedules, summaries, and exhibits, ready for negotiation, discovery, depositions, or trial.

About

Michael Galdys, MBA, CFE

Founder · Former IRS Criminal Investigation Supervisory Special Agent

Michael Galdys is a former Supervisory Special Agent with IRS Criminal Investigation (IRS-CI), the federal law enforcement agency that investigates tax crimes, money laundering, and other financial crimes. As a supervisor, he led special agents conducting complex financial investigations, work that depends on following money through bank records, business books, and layers of transactions.

He founded Guardian Edge Forensics to bring that same investigative discipline to private matters: helping attorneys and their clients trace assets, uncover undisclosed income, and turn complex financial records into clear, well-supported findings.

Michael holds an MBA and is a Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE).
