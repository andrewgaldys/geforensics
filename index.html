Financial investigations and litigation support

Guardian Edge Forensics traces assets and analyzes financial records for attorneys and their clients in divorce, fraud, and tax matters. The firm uses AI technology to make that work faster and more accurate.

Founded by Michael Galdys, MBA, CFE, a former Supervisory Special Agent with IRS Criminal Investigation.

Services

Divorce and family law

  • Tracing marital and separate funds
  • Finding undisclosed income, accounts, and assets
  • Lifestyle analysis: comparing a spouse's spending with the income they report
  • Reviewing the books of a business owned by either spouse

Fraud and tax matters

  • Embezzlement and employee theft
  • Misuse of business or family funds
  • Net worth and bank deposits analyses to estimate unreported income
  • Financial analysis for attorneys handling tax and white-collar cases

Records review and case support

  • Reviewing bank and brokerage statements and flagging unexplained transfers
  • Turning financial records into schedules, summaries, and charts
  • Spotting gaps in what has been produced in discovery
  • Witness interviews
  • Helping counsel prepare for depositions

Guardian Edge Forensics uses AI technology to organize and cross-check large volumes of bank, brokerage, and business records. That speeds up the review and helps catch transactions that are easy to miss by hand. Michael checks every finding against the source documents, and each figure in a schedule or chart is tied back to the record it came from.

If your matter turns on financial records and you're not sure it fits, call and ask.

About Michael Galdys

Michael Galdys is a former Supervisory Special Agent with IRS Criminal Investigation (IRS-CI), the law enforcement arm of the Internal Revenue Service. IRS-CI investigates federal tax, money laundering, and other financial crimes, and as a Supervisory Special Agent, Michael oversaw agents working those cases.

He founded Guardian Edge Forensics to bring that experience to civil and private matters, working with attorneys, business owners, and individuals.

Michael holds an MBA and is a Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE).
